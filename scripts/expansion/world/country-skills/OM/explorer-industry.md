# OM Explorer Industry - Oman Datacenter Press, Vendor, and Market Discovery

Date: 2026-08-12. Scope: Sultanate of Oman (OM). Repo divisions: South Batina; North Batina; Buraymi; Interior; Muscat; Musandam; Southeastern; Northeastern; Central; Dhahira; Dhofar. Official aliases: Al Batinah South, Al Batinah North, Al Buraimi, Ad Dakhiliyah, Muscat, Musandam, Ash Sharqiyah South, Ash Sharqiyah North, Al Wusta, Ad Dhahirah, Dhofar.

Reliability grades: **A** = official/operator/regulator/government media; **B** = established trade press, local newspaper, contractor/customer case study, or strong cable/telecom publication; **C** = market reports, directories, social posts, syndicated PR, crypto blogs, lead-gen pages. Industry research should find leads, but final facility records should prefer A/B corroboration.

## 0. Industry Frame

- Oman has real datacenter clusters, but coverage is uneven and often uses metro labels that hide physical governorates. MC1 is marketed as Muscat but should be mapped to Barka/South Batina when physical evidence supports it. Salalah is a separate Dhofar hub, not just a cable route.
- Main clusters: Muscat/Rusayl/KOM/Bawshar/Seeb, Barka, Sohar, Salalah, Duqm, Nizwa/Firq, Jabal Al Akhdar, and Ibri for OCI Dedicated Region evidence.
- Status discipline matters. `MoU`, `JDA`, `land lease`, `groundbreaking`, `launched`, `inaugurated`, `operational`, `available`, `built to Tier III`, and `Uptime certified` are different states. Preserve the source verb.
- Cloud reality as of this review: AWS Muscat Local Zone is officially available (`me-south-1-mct-1a`). OCI Dedicated Region infrastructure exists in Oman through Otech/ITHCA/Oracle evidence, including Muscat primary and Ibri secondary. Azure and Google Cloud official region lists do not show Oman public regions. Do not treat partner sales presence as cloud-region evidence.
- Crypto/data-hosting facilities in Salalah Free Zone are relevant large-load digital infrastructure but should be tagged separately from normal colocation.
- Arabic search is mandatory for official launches and local coverage. Use Arabic operator names and stage verbs.

## 7. De-Duplication and Reliability Rules

1. One physical site can have several aliases: ODP/Otech/Oman Data Park, Omantel/Equinix JV, Ooredoo/Syntys/data2cloud, MC1 Muscat/Barka, Salalah DC/CLS.
2. Keep cloud nodes separate from facilities unless a source identifies the host site. AWS Local Zone, OCI Dedicated Region, Oracle Alloy, and sovereign cloud service claims are important leads but not rack/colocation capacity by default.
3. Separate capacities: IT MW, facility power, solar MWp, grid MVA, racks, land area, mining machines, and announced JDA MW.
4. Treat `Tier III`, `Level 3+`, `99.982%`, and `built to Tier III` as claims. Use Uptime/TIA certification lists for certified status.
5. Market reports can expand the operator list, but every candidate must be reconciled to official/operator/trade evidence before final enumeration.
6. For negative divisions, save the best English and Arabic queries used. Blank division coverage is not acceptable for Oman.
