# BN Explorer Official - Brunei Darussalam Datacenter Enumeration via Regulatory, Planning, Energy, Government-IT and Cloud-Region Sources

Date: 2026-08-12. Country: **BN Brunei Darussalam**. Scope: official / regulatory discovery for operational and proposed datacenter, colocation, cloud, government IT, IXP and cable-landing infrastructure across all **4 districts**: Brunei-Muara, Belait, Tutong, Temburong.

Reliability grades: **A** = official public-sector/regulator/permits/registry page, operator official page, listed-company filing, or cloud-provider region page; **B** = reputable trade or local business press with named parties and concrete facts; **C** = directories, marketplaces, partner pages, SEO hosting pages and marketing pages that do not disclose facility-level evidence; **U** = not independently verified. A grade applies only to the supported fact. Example: an operator page proves the operator claims a facility/service; it does not prove MW, Uptime certification, or permits unless it says so or a primary registry confirms it.

---

## 0. Structural Facts and Caveats

- **Administrative coverage is complete at 4 districts.** Brunei is divided into Brunei-Muara (capital district, includes Bandar Seri Begawan, Gadong, Tungku, Berakas, Jerudong), Belait (Kuala Belait, Seria, Lumut; oil-and-gas heartland), Tutong (Tutong town, Telisai), and Temburong (eastern exclave, largely protected rainforest). The world-manifest divisions are `Belait`, `Brunei-Muara`, `Temburong`, `Tutong`. Always log all four rows even when negative.
- **Tiny, state-led market.** Brunei's telecom and digital infrastructure was consolidated in 2019-2020 into **Unified National Networks (UNN)**, a state-owned wholesale network company, absorbing the assets of TelBru, DST, Progresif and Brunei International Gateway (BIG) plus submarine cable assets (DCD analysis, 2026-01-16). Datacenter activity is overwhelmingly concentrated in **Brunei-Muara**; Tutong hosts a cable landing station and a satellite earth station; Belait and Temburong are negative sweeps for commercial datacenters.
- **No public national datacenter registry exists.** Build the inventory from: AITI licences; EGNC (E-Government National Centre) government-datacenter pages; UNN / DST / imagine / BIG official pages; submarine cable landing records (Tungku in Brunei-Muara, Telisai in Tutong); electricity records (DES, Berakas Power Company); procurement/tenders; cloud-provider region pages; and trade press.
- **Policy frame.** Wawasan 2035 ("Smart Nation" vision), the **Digital Economy Masterplan 2025** (first five-year blueprint, published June 2020), and **Digital Brunei 2030** (announced 2026-06-02 by MTIC; overseen by the **Digital Brunei Council**, renamed from the Digital Economy Council in August 2024). Digital Brunei 2030 names future national projects including an AI-capable data centre and sovereign cloud, but the announcement is a policy pipeline source, not evidence of a specific live facility address, rack count, or MW.
- **Language.** English and Malay (Bahasa Melayu) cover official sources; the Malay term for datacenter is "pusat data". Most official pages are bilingual or English-first.
- **Status honesty.** UNN's Tungku prefab datacenter was under construction from 2023 and expected operational in early 2024; no standalone launch press release was found in this pass (2026-08-12). However, UNN's live colocation page now lists `BRUDC4 Tungku Prefab DC`, `BRUDC2 Sumbiling Telhouse DC`, and `BRUDC3 Tungku DC`, and DST signed a colocation resale agreement (2025-12-02) referencing Tungku Zone 8 and Sumbiling Telephone House. Record "operational/marketed service" from the operator service page, while keeping the absence of a launch announcement visible.

## 2. Legal and Regulatory Anchors

- **Telecom regulation.** AITI regulates under the Telecommunications Order, 2001, and grants individual licences to facility/network-based operators (imagine/TelBru, DST, Progresif, NiAT, BIG). A datacenter colocation business as such has no separate public licence class in Brunei; colocation/cloud is delivered by licensed telecom players (UNN wholesale; DST/imagine resale) or by the government (EGNC). Check AITI's Licences pages for any new "data centre" category before assuming.
- **Data protection.** AITI ran a public consultation on personal data protection for the private sector and published a response in December 2021, but do not treat provider "data residency" language as a statutory rule without checking the current AITI/PDP materials and any new legislation. UNN's "hosted in Brunei" and local data-residency wording is an operator claim unless a live legal requirement is cited.
- **Cybersecurity.** National cybersecurity capacity is coordinated through government channels (Cyber Security Brunei context); no public datacenter-specific security certification register was found. AITI publishes a voluntary Guide on AI Governance and Ethics (Second Edition) - policy context, not a facility record.
- **Planning and building.** Ministry of Development / Town and Country Planning / Building Control sources are the expected route for development permission, but no public online planning-permission search portal was found for Brunei. Planning/building evidence usually has to come from operator news, procurement releases, or official project announcements. Treat this trail as low-yield and do not cite Wikipedia as planning evidence.
- **Electricity.** DES runs generation/transmission/distribution and connects large loads; BPC (Berakas Power Company) is a separate GLC supplying Brunei-Muara strategic sites. DCD reports a second independent power feed for the Tungku DC arranged with BPC (2023). No public connection register; use press for connection milestones.
- **Investment promotion.** BDEB (Brunei Economic Development Board) and InvestBrunei promote foreign investment; no BN datacenter-specific incentive announcement was confirmed in this pass - check their sites before assuming any DC incentive exists.

## 4. Official Verification Workflow

1. Start with a candidate name/address from industry sources (UNN, DST, imagine, BIG, EGNC, Tech Greencloud).
2. Confirm the operator's licence/status via AITI pages (licences, guidelines) - proves licensed operator existence, not a facility.
3. Search UNN official pages for facility claims: `BRUDC4 Tungku Prefab DC`, `BRUDC2 Sumbiling Telhouse DC`, `BRUDC3 Tungku DC`, Tungku Zone 8, colocation/cloud hosting, and Borneo-IX. Record exact wording and date.
4. Search EGNC pages and the current service catalogue for government/private colocation and shared-services claims; note the Gadong (Jalan E-Kerajaan, Simpang 69-18) address as the government IT campus.
5. Check the energy trail: DES and energy.gov.bn for grid/power context; BPC pages and press for the Tungku second power feed.
6. Check submarine cable landing records (Tungku: SMW3/AAG/LBC/ALC; Telisai: SJC) as infrastructure anchors - never as datacenter census entries by themselves.
7. Check cloud-provider official region pages for Brunei presence/absence (negative as of 2026-08-12).
8. Grade every field independently; do not promote directory rack counts, MW, addresses, or Uptime/ISO claims unless operator/official/registry evidence supports them.

## 7. Reliability Rules Specific to Brunei

- Treat `designed to Uptime Tier IV (electrical) / Tier III (mechanical) standards`, `Tier 3 requirements`, and `Tier 3 Uptime Certified Design` language as **operator/vendor design or compliance claims**; only an Uptime Institute registry entry proves certification.
- Treat `ISO 27001 certified` (UNN cloud hosting + colocation claim) as an operator claim until a certificate/registry reference is captured.
- Treat cable landing stations (Tungku, Telisai), the Borneo-IX, and the NiAT satellite earth station as **digital-infrastructure anchors, not datacenters**.
- Treat `cloud` as a local hosted service unless an AWS/Azure/GCP/OCI official region page says otherwise. No BN public region found on 2026-08-12.
- Treat directory listings (DataCenterMap, Datacenters.com, Inflect, Cloudscene) as discovery leads only (C).
- Do not fabricate MW or rack counts: BN facilities rarely publish power figures. The strongest public sizing is UNN Tungku phase-1 = 200 racks / 51,000 sq ft complex; EGNC publishes rack product details, not a total facility rack count.
- For addresses, prefer: operator official page > government/EGNC page > reputable local press > directories.
- Record negative searches for Belait and Temburong explicitly; false positives are rare but "data centre" appears in unrelated contexts (e.g., distribution centres, iftar centres).
- After the June 2026 cabinet reshuffle, re-verify ministry ownership of EGNC/AITI/MTIC before citing structure.
