## 2026-08-11（refresh）
- status 更新：从“媒体报道称获准申请 CUP”提升为官方 county record 可核实的 `local process`。Williams County Board of County Commissioners 2025-02-18 minutes 记载，Scott Bennett/Teton Digital 就液冷 facility 做 presentation，委员会以 4-0 recorded vote “approve the appeal for the data center facility and not the battery storage”。来源：https://williamsnd.civicweb.net/document/177538/
- 前置官方记录补证：Williams County Development Services 2025-01-24 denial letter 说明，基于 2025-01-22 pre-application meeting，Teton Digital 的 CUP 申请因 1-mile sensitive-property setback 不满足而不能受理；若相邻 crypto-currency mining data center 获批，也可能不满足 3-mile data-center separation。来源：https://williamsnd.civicweb.net/document/176705/Jan%20Bergstrom%20Teton%20Digital%20denial%20letter%20%281%29.pdf?handle=CCBD471C6A6F4FD58ED41A25BD0F9B16
- capacity/owner/location 补齐：Williams County agenda packet one-pager（Teton Digital, LLC | February 2025）描述 Trinity 1 位于 Dry Fork, North Dakota，25 acres adjacent to existing MWEC substation，100MW+ liquid cooled data center project，Phase 1 为 30MW（Q3/Q4 2025 target），Phase 2 为 100MW with new company-built substation（Q3/Q4 2026 target），Phase I investment $45MM、Phase II $155MM、total $200MM+。来源：https://williamsnd.civicweb.net/document/177085/
- owner/developer 当前口径：Teton Digital 当前 projects page 将 North Dakota 列为 100 MW Tier 3 campus，称 “Phase II Conditional Use Permit secured (70 MW); Phase I in final permitting (30 MW)”；同页又把 DCD 报道描述为 “Phase II conditional use permit approval”。该说法为公司来源，本次未找到后续 county CUP/building permit/inspection/CO/energization 公开记录与之独立匹配。来源：https://www.tetondigital.io/projects
- 第三方补充：KFYR 2025-02-18 报道 commissioners authorized Teton Digital to begin work toward applying for a CUP，项目为 Tioga 以南约 8 miles 的 100 MW facility，phase one hoped operational in late 2025 pending approval；DCD 2025-02-20 报道同一事项并给出 25-acre/80-acre parcel、30MW phase one、100MW+ substation/full facility target。来源：https://www.kfyrtv.com/2025/02/18/williams-county-commissioners-give-go-ahead-another-data-center-company-apply-conditional-use-permit/ ; https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/teton-digital-gets-go-ahead-for-100mw-data-center-in-north-dakota/
- 行业 tracker 当前口径：Baxtel 把 Teton Digital Dry Fork Township 标为 multi-phase construction project，site planned for 2027 with expansions through 2028；Cleanview 标为 planned、100 MW、expected 2026。二者未提供比 county minutes/packet 更强的一手 permit 或 construction evidence。来源：https://baxtel.com/data-center/teton-digital-dry-fork-township-nd ; https://cleanview.co/data-centers/north-dakota/918/project-trinity
- 冲突/证据不足：公司称 Phase II CUP secured 与 Phase I final permitting，但本次 public official/local-gov-first 搜索只核实到 appeal approval，不足以认定 construction/energized/live；county one-pager 的 Phase 2 “100MW” 与公司当前 “Phase II 70MW + Phase I 30MW” 口径存在 phase sizing 差异，data.json 暂按 100 MW total planned、30/70 phasing as owner-reported current framing 处理。
