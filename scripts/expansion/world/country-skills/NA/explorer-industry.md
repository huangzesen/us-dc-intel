# NA Explorer Industry - Namibia Datacenter Enumeration

Date: 2026-08-12. Country: Namibia (NA). Scope: industry, trade-press, operator, vendor, cloud, subsea, and market-intelligence discovery. Pair this file with `explorer-official.md`; this file creates leads, while the official file promotes or rejects them.

Reliability grades:
- A: official operator/vendor page, official cloud-region page, regulator/municipality/Gazette/government source.
- B: reputable press or trade source with named operator, place, date, and project details.
- C: aggregator, social media, consultant/market slides, broad market commentary, or unsourced local claims.

## 0. Market Frame

Namibia is a small carrier-led data-centre market. The confirmed commercial/service evidence is concentrated around:
- Khomas: Windhoek/Brakwater, especially Paratus Armada and Telecom Namibia Infinitum co-location.
- Erongo: Swakopmund/Walvis Bay, especially cable landing infrastructure and Paratus/Telecom Namibia Equiano activity.

As of this methodology date, no official AWS, Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud, Oracle, or Huawei hyperscale region page names Namibia. Treat "Namibia cloud region", "hyperscale Namibia", or "AI data centre Namibia" claims as unverified until matched to an official provider, permit, grid, or Gazette record.

Industry discovery should focus on:
1. Operator pages and annual reports.
2. Trade press covering Paratus, Telecom Namibia, Equiano, WACS, 2Africa, SARSSy, and fibre routes.
3. Local business press for construction, building-plan, budget, tender, and regulator stories.
4. Energy/hydrogen/renewable announcements that could become compute siting leads.
5. Aggregators only as lead generators.

## 2. Known Leads and How to Treat Them

### Paratus Armada Data Center - Windhoek/Brakwater, Khomas

Status: confirmed operator/service lead.

Use A-grade Paratus pages for service existence. Use B-grade press for detail such as launch timing, Brakwater campus, N$123m investment, two colocation halls, and energy centres. Useful corroborating searches:
```text
"Paratus launches Namibian data center in Windhoek"
"Paratus" "Armada" "Brakwater"
"Paratus to invest N$123m in data centre"
"Work on Windhoek Paratus data centre progressing well"
```

Official follow-up: City of Windhoek/Brakwater planning, MEFT/EIA, ECB/NamPower/City Electricity records, CRAN licence evidence, BIPA legal entity.

### Telecom Namibia Infinitum Co-location - Windhoek, Khomas

Status: confirmed operator product/service lead.

Use Telecom Namibia Infinitum pages as A-grade evidence for co-location service. DataCenterMap-style "Telecom Namibia Datacenter" pages are C only.

Useful searches:
```text
"Telecom Namibia" "Infinitum" "co-location"
"Telecom Namibia" "data centre" "Windhoek"
"Telecom Namibia" "Data Centre of the Government"
"Telecom Namibia" "annual report" "data centre"
```

Official follow-up: Telecom Namibia annual reports/tenders, CRAN licensee list, Windhoek building/power records if a discrete facility can be identified.

### Swakopmund Cable Landing / Data-Centre Infrastructure - Erongo

Status: confirmed cable-landing infrastructure; data-centre/colocation status must be fact-specific.

Key industry trail:
- Equiano landed at Swakopmund in 2022 through Paratus/Telecom Namibia arrangements.
- DCD reported Telecom Namibia inaugurated/activated its Equiano cable landing station at Swakopmund in 2024.
- Submarine Networks and ITWeb Africa carry useful landing-station chronology.
- 2Africa official site is authoritative for the system, but not by itself proof of a Namibia colocation facility.

Useful searches:
```text
"Equiano" "Swakopmund" "Paratus"
"Telecom Namibia inaugurates Equiano cable landing station"
"Paratus Swakopmund landing station ready for Equiano cable"
"2Africa" "Namibia" "Swakopmund"
"SARSSy" "Equiano" "Telecom Namibia" "Swakopmund"
```

Official follow-up: CRAN, Swakopmund Municipality, MEFT/EIA, NamPower/Erongo RED, Gazette.

### Government / National Data Centre

Status: lead, not a confirmed new facility unless site/procurement/budget evidence is found.

Press and procurement references mention a government data centre and future National Data Centre plans. Promote only when tied to an official strategic plan, budget vote, tender, award, or site.

## 5. Triage Rules

- Promote immediately to official workflow if a source names an operator plus a site/town and any of: MW/MVA, racks, halls, building work, EIA, licence, tender, or cable landing station.
- Keep as C if the claim is only "cloud", "digital transformation", "ICT infrastructure", or "data sovereignty" without a facility.
- For "first/largest" claims, check dates. Paratus Armada launch and Telecom Namibia Infinitum can cause later stories to double-count existing capacity.
- Record cable landing stations under a separate facility type unless customer colocation is explicit.
- Always run the Khomas and Erongo official follow-up before opening a new Namibia project record.
