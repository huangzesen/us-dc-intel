- Planned: per-canton (or per-division) skill files covering the canton's official permit route (eBaugesucheZH, SAD/SITG, FAO/CAMAC, eBau, UDC, Baupublikationen), official gazette, local utility (EKS/ewz/EKZ/AEW/BKW/SIG/Romande Energie/IWB/CKW/WWZ), and priority operator/place pivots per canton.
