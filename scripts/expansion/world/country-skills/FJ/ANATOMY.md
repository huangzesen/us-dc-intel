## Division layer (future)

The manifest (`world-manifest.jsonl`) defines Fiji as **5 divisions/dependency**: **Central, Eastern, Northern, Rotuma, Western**. A future `divisions/` layer will carry per-division discovery/audit skills; locality mapping matters (Suva/Vatuwaqa = Central; Savusavu/Labasa = Northern; Natadola/Nadi/Lautoka/Sigatoka/Denarau = Western; Rotuma separate), and low-yield Eastern and Rotuma must still be searched and recorded as covered.
