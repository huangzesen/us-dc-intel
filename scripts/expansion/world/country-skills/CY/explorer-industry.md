# CY Explorer Industry - Cyprus Datacentre Enumeration via Operators, Trade Press, Directories, IXP/Subsea and Locality Query Patterns

Date: 2026-08-12. Scope: Cyprus (CY), all 6 districts: Nicosia/Lefkosia, Limassol/Lemesos, Larnaca/Larnaka, Famagusta/Ammochostos, Paphos/Pafos, Kyrenia/Keryneia. Angle: operator, trade-press, directory, IXP, cable and locality-led discovery. Use `explorer-official.md` to promote or reject every industry lead.

Reliability grades: **A** = operator official page, public-sector page, regulator/permit source, stock-exchange/company filing, or Uptime Institute registry; **B** = reputable trade/local press with named parties and concrete facts; **C** = directory/marketplace/SEO hosting page/partner listing; **U** = unverified. Grade each field independently.

---

## 0. Market Shape

Cyprus is a small, service-provider-led datacenter market. Nicosia and Limassol are the primary clusters; Larnaca is material because of LCA1 and Pentaskhinos; Famagusta and Paphos are mainly cable-landing contexts; Kyrenia requires TRNC/north-Cyprus treatment and has no ROC source coverage.

Confirmed or high-confidence public leads as of 2026-08-12: Cyta Nicosia/Aglantzia and Limassol/Amathounta; Cyta acquisitions/agreements for Simplex LCA1 in Larnaca and RedMax in Latsia/Nicosia; CloudLayer8/CL8 LIM in Limassol with an Uptime Institute registry entry; Logosnet DataFort in Nicosia; PrimeTel colocation across four Cyprus locations; Cablenet Engomi/Nicosia as a directory-led facility requiring operator confirmation; IXPs CyIX and JumboIX; Cyta cable landing stations at Ayia Napa, Pentaskhinos and Yeroskipos.

False-positive controls: local VPS/cloud marketing is not a public cloud region; IXPs and cable landing stations are not datacenters; Logicom is a data-centre integrator unless an owned facility is separately proven; Cablenet office address is not facility proof; north-Cyprus hosting pages need primary TRNC/operator evidence and a physical address.

## 5. IXP, Peering and Subsea Pipeline

- **CyIX / Cyprus Internet Exchange**: PeeringDB https://www.peeringdb.com/ix/487 lists Cyprus Internet Exchange in Nicosia, operated by CYTA. ISOC Pulse country page https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/ixp-tracker/country/CY/ reports 2 active IXPs in Cyprus as of August 2026. This is IXP evidence, not a colo-facility record.
- **JumboIX Cyprus**: PeeringDB https://www.peeringdb.com/ix/3541 lists JumboIX Cyprus in Limassol; PeeringDB facility https://www.peeringdb.com/fac/3055 lists IPTP, Kermia 1 Limassol, Cyprus. IPTP page https://www.iptp.net/connectivity-services/jumbix/jumboix-cyprus-pricing-plans/ says future access is planned for Nicosia, Larnaca, Paphos and Paralimni. Grade PeeringDB as B for IXP/facility context, IPTP as A/C depending on whether it is an operator-owned page or marketing claim.
- **Cyta/Cytaglobal cable landing stations**: Cytaglobal https://cytaglobal.com/en/submarine-cables says TEFKROS and KINYRAS interconnect Ayia Napa, Pentaskhinos and Yeroskipos. Submarine Networks https://www.submarinenetworks.com/en/stations/europe/cyprus independently summarizes the same three Cyta cable landing stations. Treat as A/B infrastructure context.
- **KIMONAS**: Submarine Networks https://www.submarinenetworks.com/en/systems/asia-europe-africa/kimonas describes a Cyta-owned fiber pair between Pentaskhinos and Chania; cross-check against Cytaglobal for A-grade operator statement.
- **Medusa**: official https://medusascs.com/ and Submarine Networks https://www.submarinenetworks.com/en/systems/asia-europe-africa/medusa. Cyprus is in the country list, but the exact Cyprus landing district must be checked before assigning a district.
- **UGARIT 2**: 2026-08-11 press-release mirrors and SubTel Forum report a Pentaskhinos-Tartous system with CYTA, UNIFI and Syrian Telecom. Grade B until a Cyta/project primary page is captured.

## 8. Reliability and Promotion Rules

- Promote an industry lead to **A** only when operator, regulator, permit, land, procurement, listed-company filing, cloud-provider official page, or Uptime registry evidence supports the specific fact.
- Keep address, rack count, white space, MW, certification and operational status as separate fields with separate grades.
- Keep cable landing stations and IXPs in infrastructure tables unless they are physically part of a documented colocation/datacenter facility.
- Record negative searches. For Famagusta ROC part, Paphos and Kyrenia, negative evidence is important because false positives are common.
- Use `evidence_date=2026-08-12` for this reviewed pass; refresh dates when rechecked.
