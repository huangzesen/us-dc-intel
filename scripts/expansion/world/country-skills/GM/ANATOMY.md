## Division layer (future)

The manifest (`world-manifest.jsonl`) defines The Gambia as **6 city/divisions**: **Banjul; Lower River; Central River; North Bank; Upper River; Western**. A future `divisions/` layer will carry per-division discovery/audit skills; note that the Greater Banjul Area straddles **Banjul** and **Western** (the two divisions must not be collapsed), and the main urban belt including Kanifing/Serekunda, Abuko, Bakau, Brikama, and Yundum normalises to Western.
