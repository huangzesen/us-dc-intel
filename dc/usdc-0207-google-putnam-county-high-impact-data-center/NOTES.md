- status/capacity: refreshed from early-stage state announcement to `local process`; capacity remains null. The WV HIDC statute defines a High Impact Data Center as 90 MW or more of aggregate critical IT load, but no project-specific MW was found, so this threshold is noted only as a statutory minimum and not treated as confirmed capacity. Sources: https://governor.wv.gov/article/governor-morrisey-announces-google-data-center-project-putnam-county ; https://code.wvlegislature.gov/11-6N-2/
- owner/location: set owner to Google and location city to Buffalo, Putnam County, WV. PCDA stated on 2026-03-27 that Google confirmed it acquired land in Buffalo for a potential data center campus and was continuing due diligence; Google’s Buffalo project site says it is advancing plans for a data center campus in Buffalo. Sources: https://pcda.org/pcda-statement-google-data-center/ ; https://www.buffalowvdatacenter.com/
- state process: added WV Department of Commerce / WVDED HIDC certification guidance as context. The guidance says complete submissions enter a 14-day statutory review and require location, parcel count/acreage, critical IT load, in-service date, utility, water, and environmental/community information, but it does not publish Google’s underlying submission or order. Source: https://westvirginia.gov/datacenters/begincertification/
- company/community update: Google’s Buffalo site says it plans coordination with the West Virginia State Historic Preservation Office, will pay for necessary electrical infrastructure, and describes water replenishment/workforce commitments. It does not disclose parcels, MW, construction start, permits, or an approved site plan. Source: https://www.buffalowvdatacenter.com/
