## 2026-08-11（refresh）
- Status updated from `announced/planned` to `site work-construction`. Google officially announced on 2025-10-02 a $4B Arkansas investment through 2027 including its first Arkansas data center in West Memphis; Arkansas EDC's same-day release says Google is constructing the advanced data center campus on a site of more than 1,000 acres. Sources: https://blog.google/company-news/inside-google/company-announcements/google-american-innovation-arkansas/ ; https://www.arkansasedc.com/news-events/newsroom/detail/2025/10/02/google-locating-new-data-center-in-west-memphis--arkansas-with-multi-billion-dollar-investment
- Owner clarified as Google, with Groot LLC retained as the permit applicant / project entity. USACE Memphis District public notice MVM-2024-216 identifies applicant Groot LLC and the stated purpose as a five-building data center campus with supporting office buildings, parking, internal roads, utility substation, stormwater facilities, utilities, and associated infrastructure. Source: https://www.mvm.usace.army.mil/Portals/51/MVM%202024-216.pdf
- Location enriched from USACE public notice: 1,178-acre parcel at the south extent of Bollinger Road, West Memphis, Crittenden County, Arkansas, latitude 35.108877 and longitude -90.216323. USACE describes 920 acres in project scope, with the main campus on about 580 acres and the eastern section used for borrow material. Source date: public notice MVM-2024-216, reviewed 2026-08-11. Source: https://www.mvm.usace.army.mil/Portals/51/MVM%202024-216.pdf
- Power evidence updated. Google says it is collaborating with Entergy on a new 600 MW solar project and peak-load-reduction programs; Entergy says the facility will be served by existing generation/transmission bolstered by a 600 MW solar project and 350 MW battery storage in Jefferson County, with Google paying rates covering the resource. Sources: https://blog.google/company-news/inside-google/company-announcements/google-american-innovation-arkansas/ ; https://www.entergy.com/entergy-arkansas-powers-googles-4b-investment-in-the-state
- APSC regulatory status added: Entergy's investor regulatory page and APSC dockets show the Google special rate contract (25-055-P) was approved on 2026-01-29 and the Cypress Solar CECPN (25-054-U) was approved on 2026-03-03. Sources: https://investors.entergy.com/investors/regulatory-and-other-information-1/regulatory-and-other-information/default.aspx ; https://apps.apsc.arkansas.gov/olsv2/docket_search_results.asp?CaseNumber=25-055-P ; https://apps.apsc.arkansas.gov/olsv2/docket_search_results.asp?CaseNumber=25-054-U
