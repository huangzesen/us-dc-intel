## Division layer (future)

The manifest (`world-manifest.jsonl`) defines the project-required 8-division working model for Guinea: **Boke; Conakry; Kindia; Faranah; Kankan; Labe; Mamou; Nzerekore**, corresponding to Guinea's seven administrative regions plus the special zone/governorate of Conakry. A future `divisions/` layer will carry per-division discovery/audit skills; records use the ASCII manifest names for attribution with French/local spellings kept as aliases, and facilities are attributed by physical site rather than by the ministry name appearing in an older article.
