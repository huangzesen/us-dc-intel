## Division layer (future)

Per `world-manifest.jsonl`, KP is modeled as **capital city/province/special city/metropolitan city** with exactly **13 divisions = 1 capital city (Pyongyang) + 9 provinces (South Pyongan, North Pyongan, Chagang, South Hwanghae, North Hwanghae, Kangwon, South Hamgyong, North Hamgyong, Ryanggang) + 1 metropolitan city (Nampo) + 2 special cities (Rason, Kaesong)**. Pyongyang is the only credible IT cluster; most provinces are negative or carry only network/OSINT leads. When the division layer is built, keep the low-exposure boundary (city/province context only; no precise coordinates, security layouts, personnel, or targeting inferences) and the sanctions/ROK-government/state-media source separation intact.
