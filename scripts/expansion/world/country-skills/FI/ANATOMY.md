## Division layer (future)

- Finland enumerates region-first with municipality drill-down; building permits are municipal (kaavoitus/asemakaava zoning, rakennuslupa/rakentamislupa), so per-region search must route through municipal domains, Lupapiste/julkipano, YVA pages (ymparisto.fi), and local DSO/district-heating companies.
- Planned: per-region (or per-division) skill files covering municipal planning/building-control patterns, YVA/ELY routing, Fingrid + DSO/district-heating pivots (Fortum/Helen/Vantaa Energy/Lahti Energia/Tampereen Energia/Haminan Energia/KSS), and priority operator/place pivots per region.
