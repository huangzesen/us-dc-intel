Sources: https://www.yorkcountysc.gov/1313/Data-Centers (crawled 2026-08-11); https://q.com/data-centers/york/ (crawled 2026-08-11).

Source: https://www.yorkcountysc.gov/1313/Data-Centers (crawled 2026-08-11).
- development scope updated: QTS states the campus is nine buildings over three phases; phase one is four buildings across approximately 109 acres; the planned investment for the currently approved campus is approximately $8 billion; the project page lists 1,000 construction jobs and 200 permanent jobs. Source: https://q.com/data-centers/york/ (crawled 2026-08-11).
- location/owner updated: QTS project page lists the location as 2143 Hands Mill Hwy, Rock Hill, SC 29745; owner/operator field updated from null to QTS Data Centers. Source: https://q.com/data-centers/york/ (crawled 2026-08-11).
- construction milestone added: Gilbane announced on 2026-06-30 that structural steel for the first building in the initial phase had topped out and that construction of the QTS campus was ongoing. Source: https://www.gilbaneco.com/about/whats-new/news/gilbane-marks-steel-topping-out-milestone-for-data-center-campus-in-upstate-south-carolina/.
- capacity remains null: QTS says it has a power agreement with York Electric Cooperative for nine buildings over three phases but does not disclose specific power capacity for security/confidentiality reasons. Source: https://q.com/data-centers/york/ (crawled 2026-08-11).

These support permitting/site readiness but are not CO, energization, or in-service proof. Sources: https://www.yorkcountysc.gov/1313/Data-Centers; https://q.com/data-centers/york/.
